fix(pipeline-stall+tp-state): move ledger advance before _alert(); persist state after TP placement

1. PIPELINE_STALL: _alert() internally calls CommunicationsOfficer.check_phase_stall()
   which creates a fresh ledger in ENTRY_CONFIRMED state. My previous fix placed
   the ledger advance AFTER _alert(), so the stall check always saw ENTRY_CONFIRMED
   and fired PIPELINE_STALL even after successful recovery. Fixed: advance ledger
   AND save state BEFORE _alert() in both Binance and DeepCoin recover paths.

2. XAU TP=0 on restart: The open flow did not persist state between TP placement
   (_smart_realign_defenses) and the sentinel loop. A VPS restart/interrupt between
   those two points left the state file with stale/zero initial_qty and tv_tps,
   causing restart recovery to compute TP qty=0 and place no TP limits.
   Fixed: add _save_state() immediately after _smart_realign_defenses() in the
   open flow (both Binance and DeepCoin supervisors).
